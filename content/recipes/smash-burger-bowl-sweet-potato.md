Put it in the fridge for at least 10 minutes so the flavors blend."
  - title: "Prep the vegetables"
    minutes: 4
    text: "Still while the oven heats, slice 1/4 onion for the mushrooms and finely dice another 1/4 onion for the toppings and beans. Chop 2 cups romaine, dice 1 small tomato, and slice the mushrooms if they are whole."
  - title: "Roast the sweet potato"
    minutes: 25
    text: "As soon as the oven is hot, put the pan in and roast for about 25 minutes, until both sides are browned and a fork slides in easily. Set a timer and flip the rounds once halfway through."
  - title: "Get the burgers ready"
    minutes: 2
    text: "While the sweet potato roasts, roll 6 oz 90% lean ground beef into 2 loose balls and keep them in the fridge until cooking. Cut 1 slice American cheese in half, and cut 2 squares of parchment paper about 6 inches wide."
  - title: "Simmer the beans"
    minutes: 6
    text: "Put 1/2 cup rinsed pinto beans, 1 tbsp ketchup, 1 tsp mustard, 1 tbsp of the diced onion, 1/4 tsp smoked paprika, a pinch of garlic powder, 1/2 tsp maple syrup if you like, and 2 tbsp water in a small pot over medium-low heat.   - title: "Start the mushrooms and onions"
    minutes: 2
    text: "Heat a large skillet, preferably cast iron, over medium-high heat. Add 1 cup sliced cremini mushrooms and the sliced onion to the dry pan in a single layer, and leave them alone for 2 minutes."
  - title: "Finish the mushrooms and onions"
    minutes: 4
    text: "Add 1/2 tsp oil and stir for 3 to 4 minutes, until browned. Stir in 1 tsp Worcestershire or low-sodium soy sauce for the last 30 seconds, then move them to a plate."
  - title: "Set up the cold side"

  - title: "Let the crust form"
    minutes: 2

    minutes: 1

  - title: "Build the bowl"
    minutes: 1
    text: "Put the sweet potato, patties, and mushrooms and onions on the empty half of the bowl. Drizzle the sauce over everything and serve the beans on the side."

This is the oven version of the Smash Burger Bowl, for a kitchen with big sweet potatoes and no air fryer. A big sweet potato is too large to microwave and smash whole, so it is cut into rounds and roasted, which takes about 10 minutes longer.

The bowl is built in two halves on purpose. The hot sweet potato, patties, and mushrooms go on one side and the cold romaine, tomato, onion, and pickles go on the other, so the lettuce stays crisp and every bite has the hot and cold contrast that makes a burger satisfying.

Lean beef needs help to smash well, so the pan gets very hot, a little oil goes in first, and parchment keeps the meat from sticking to the spatula. The dark crust that forms in the first two untouched minutes is where most of the flavor comes from.
